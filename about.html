About us

Good software,
built for people.

The Good Software Foundation is an independent non-profit that equips people from underserved and minority communities with technology skills, and backs the tools their communities ask for.

Mission

To equip people from underserved and minority communities with technology skills and connect them with community-defined needs, creating a cycle of learning, building, and lasting service.

Contact us

We would love to hear from you.

Whether you want to learn with us, build with us, or have a project your community needs, reach out.
