Who we are

We teach builders, and back the tools their communities ask for.

The Good Software Foundation is an independent non-profit. We exist because the most important software in the world is often the least funded: the app that connects a congregation to its place of worship, the bookkeeping tool a small non-profit trusts with every donation, the systems a community school runs on.

We work in three ways. We run free and low-cost workshops and mentorship for new builders. We provide a platform that connects community organizations with open source volunteers and enables community sponsorship. And we fund the maintainers who keep the results running.

Everything we support is open source, auditable and free to use. We take no equity, we sell no data, and we work directly with community organizations to identify their needs.
