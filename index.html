Who we are

A home for software that serves the public good.

The Good Software Foundation is an independent non-profit. We exist because the most important software in the world is often the least funded: the library behind a clinic's records, the tool a teacher uses offline, the ledger a small charity trusts with every donation.

We give those projects what they usually lack — sustained funding, security review, release engineering and governance that outlives any single maintainer. Everything we support is open source, auditable and free to use. We take no equity, we sell no data, and we answer to the communities we serve.
