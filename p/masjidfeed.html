MasjidFeed

A native mobile frontend for existing mosque websites, made to keep a congregation close to the prayer hall and to one another.

{% include icon.html name="google-play" %} Get it on Google Play {% include icon.html name="app-store" %} Download on the App Store

One familiar place

The information your community needs, ready when it matters.

MasjidFeed turns a mosque's existing website into a calm, native app experience for iOS and Android. Prayer times, iqamah details, Jumu'ah schedules, announcements and upcoming events are all easy to find.

It connects through a companion WordPress integration, so each masjid can keep its own identity, content and day-to-day publishing workflow. The app can be configured to show the services that particular community offers, including donations, directions and prayer reminders.
